Voice operations are a call path plus evidence

A stable UC environment depends on clean dial plans, healthy clusters, reliable contact-center logic, vendor-aware integrations, and careful incident notes. I approach voice issues by mapping the path, collecting the trace, isolating the mismatch, and turning the fix into reusable documentation.

Trace the call path

Document the fix
